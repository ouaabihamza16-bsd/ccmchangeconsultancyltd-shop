About us

A Chesterfield consultancy for change and the environment

CCM Change Consultancy is the trading name of CCM CHANGE CONSULTANCY LTD, a management consultancy based in Chesterfield, Derbyshire. The company was incorporated in England and Wales on 3 June 2021 under company number 13437310.

We work with organisations that want to change how they operate: to remove waste, help work flow better and put in place the systems needed to manage their environmental impact. Our focus is sustainable business transformation, which means improving how a business runs while reducing its effect on the environment.

The way we work is hands-on and plain-spoken. Advice is shaped around what will work in your organisation, and there is one measure of success: the result for you.

Services

How we can help

Three areas of work that support each other: changing how your organisation works, getting ready for ISO 14001, and understanding where your environmental impact comes from.

Change management

Structured, practical support to plan and deliver changes to the way your organisation works, with your people involved from the start.

  • Planning and preparing change before it begins
  • Improving flow, so work moves smoothly between people and teams
  • Getting it right first time, and finishing work properly rather than just completing it
  • Treating colleagues as internal customers

ISO 14001 preparation

Help to build an environmental management system and get your organisation ready to work towards ISO 14001 certification.

  • A starting waste review and environmental audit
  • Building the environmental management system the standard asks for
  • Preparing your team and processes for an external assessment

Please note: we help you prepare for ISO 14001. CCM is not a certification body and does not certify organisations; certification is carried out by an independent certification body of your choosing. CCM does not hold ISO 14001 certification itself.

Environmental reviews

A practical look at where waste and environmental impact build up in your business, to find efficiencies and possible cost savings and give your environmental strategy a clear starting point.

Waste

Processes
How work is done and where resources go
Property
The buildings and sites you work from
Supply chain
Your suppliers and customers
